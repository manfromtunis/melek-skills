# Draft Email

Write professional emails with proper context gathering before drafting.

## Before Writing — Always Ask:

1. **Who is the recipient?** (name, role, relationship)
2. **What platform/channel?** (Gmail, AgentMail, Lemlist, LinkedIn DM)
3. **What's the goal?** (cold outreach, follow-up, partnership, support, invoice)
4. **What tone?** (formal, casual, direct, warm)
5. **Product positioning?** (if sales-related — what angle to lead with)

Do NOT assume the platform unless stated. Do NOT assume the product angle — ask.

## Drafting Rules

- **Never send directly** — always show draft for user approval first
- **Auto-CC the user's own primary address** when sending from a dedicated outbound inbox (e.g. AgentMail), so the user gets a copy of every outbound
- Run through a humanizer pass before finalizing — no em dashes, no "I hope this finds you well"-style filler, no Rule of Three padding, vary sentence length
- Match the customer's language (FR/EN) — never write in pidgin/Darija even when the customer does, unless explicitly asked

## For Cold Outreach Specifically

- Pair with a structured cold-email skill for sequence design (subject → opener → 1-line body → CTA)
- Lead with the *outcome the buyer cares about*, not the product feature
- Skip vanity metrics in the opener; specifics > superlatives
- Always check past outreach learnings (which subject lines/openers got replies in this audience) before drafting fresh
